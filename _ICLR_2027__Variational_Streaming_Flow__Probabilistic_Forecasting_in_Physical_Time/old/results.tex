% Generated from outputs/iclr/reproduce_table20--25.
% Metric statistics use NumPy's population standard deviation (ddof=0).

  
The deterministic tables report continuous ranked
probability score (CRPS), root mean squared error (RMSE), and mean absolute
error (MAE).  The stochastic tables report relative conditional mean error
(CME), relative conditional standard-deviation error (CSE), maximum mean
discrepancy (MMD), first-order Wasserstein distance ($W_1$), and energy
distance (ED).  Runtime is the synchronized rollout-generation time, reported
as mean $\pm$ population standard deviation across seeds; timings are similar
across datasets at a fixed rollout length.  For stochastic Lorenz, the
Streaming flow (perturbation) row uses normalized Gaussian initial-state
perturbations with standard deviation $0.01$.
